Recording Retention Policy (Release 4.2)

KB-MTG-2004 | NetCore Meet 4.2 | Last updated: 2024-10-03 | Status: superseded

NOTICE: This article applies to release 4.2 and has been superseded by KB-MTG-2003. It is retained for customers still running release 4.2.

Summary

This policy defines the NetCore Meet 4.2 cloud recording retention rule for meeting recordings stored in NetCore Control Hub. In release 4.2, cloud recordings are retained for a fixed 180 days, legal hold is not available, and recording transcripts are not generated.

Applies to: NetCore Meet 4.2, NetCore Meet desktop app for Windows and macOS 4.2, NetCore Meet mobile app 4.2, NetCore Meet web client 4.2, NetCore Control Hub 4.2, Cloud recordings only

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish a uniform retention standard for NetCore Meet 4.2 cloud recordings. The policy ensures that recordings are retained for the published release 4.2 retention period and removed automatically when that period expires.

This policy was written for NetCore Meet 4.2 environments where cloud recording is enabled through NetCore Control Hub. It does not define retention behavior for local files saved outside the NetCore Meet cloud recording service.

Scope

This policy applies to all NetCore Meet 4.2 cloud recordings created by licensed users and stored in the NetCore Meet recording service. It applies regardless of whether users join from the desktop app, mobile app, or web client.

The policy covers meeting recordings and associated recording metadata visible in NetCore Control Hub 4.2, including owner, meeting title, start time, end time, recording creation time, and deletion status.

  • In scope: NetCore Meet 4.2 cloud recordings created from scheduled meetings, instant meetings, and recurring meetings.
  • In scope: administrator review of recording inventory and deletion status in NetCore Control Hub 4.2.
  • Out of scope: user-managed local files, exported recordings after download, third-party archive repositories, and compliance systems outside NetCore Meet 4.2.

Definitions

  • Cloud recording: A meeting recording stored by the NetCore Meet cloud recording service and listed in NetCore Control Hub 4.2.
  • Recording owner: The user account associated with the meeting recording in NetCore Control Hub 4.2.
  • Retention period: The fixed period during which a cloud recording remains available before automatic deletion. For NetCore Meet 4.2, the retention period is 180 days.
  • Deletion date: The date on which the NetCore Meet 4.2 recording service removes the cloud recording after the 180-day retention period expires.
  • Legal hold: A mechanism to preserve recordings beyond the normal retention period. Legal hold is not available in NetCore Meet 4.2.
  • Transcript: A text representation of meeting audio. Transcripts are not generated by NetCore Meet 4.2 cloud recording.

Policy Statements

NetCore Meet 4.2 uses a fixed retention model for cloud recordings. Administrators cannot shorten or extend the retention period in NetCore Control Hub 4.2.

Cloud recordings are retained for 180 days from the recording creation date. After 180 days, the recording is eligible for automatic deletion by the NetCore Meet recording service.

NetCore Meet 4.2 does not provide legal hold for cloud recordings. Requests to preserve content must be handled by downloading the recording before the 180-day retention period expires and storing it in an approved external repository.

NetCore Meet 4.2 does not generate transcripts for cloud recordings. Any text notes, summaries, or manually produced transcripts are outside the NetCore Meet 4.2 cloud recording service and are not governed by this recording-retention control.

  • The 180-day retention period is fixed for all NetCore Meet 4.2 cloud recordings.
  • No per-user, per-site, or per-meeting retention override is available in NetCore Control Hub 4.2.
  • No legal hold control is available in NetCore Control Hub 4.2.
  • No automatic transcripts are created for NetCore Meet 4.2 recordings.
  • Administrators should treat day 179 as the final operational review day for any recording that must be downloaded before automatic deletion.

Enforcement

The NetCore Meet 4.2 recording service enforces the 180-day retention period automatically. The retention value is not configurable in NetCore Control Hub 4.2.

Administrators should review cloud recording inventory at regular intervals and notify recording owners when business-critical recordings must be downloaded before expiration. Operational review does not change the fixed 180-day deletion behavior.

Example administrative review path in NetCore Control Hub 4.2: Meetings > Recordings > Cloud Recordings > Filter by Created Date. A typical review filter for recordings approaching expiration is Created Date from 2024-01-01 00:00 to 2024-01-02 23:59 when reviewing on 2024-06-28 for recordings nearing the 180-day limit.

  • Recordings older than the 180-day retention period must not be represented as available unless they are still visible in NetCore Control Hub 4.2 at the time of review.
  • Administrators must not promise extension, legal hold, or transcript availability for NetCore Meet 4.2 cloud recordings.
  • Downloaded copies are controlled by the repository where they are stored after export, not by the NetCore Meet 4.2 cloud recording retention policy.

Exceptions

NetCore Meet 4.2 does not support retention exceptions inside the cloud recording service. The 180-day retention period applies uniformly to all NetCore Meet 4.2 cloud recordings.

If a department requires a recording for longer than 180 days, the recording owner or administrator must download the recording before the retention period expires and transfer it to an approved records repository. That action creates a separate copy outside the NetCore Meet 4.2 cloud recording service.

  • No legal hold exception is available in NetCore Meet 4.2.
  • No administrator override is available to extend retention beyond 180 days in NetCore Control Hub 4.2.
  • No transcript exception is available because transcripts are not generated by NetCore Meet 4.2.
  • Requests received after the recording has been deleted under the 180-day policy cannot be fulfilled from NetCore Meet 4.2 cloud recording storage.
